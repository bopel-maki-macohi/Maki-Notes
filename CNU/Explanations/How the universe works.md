## Dark Elements

Dark elements are elements with magical properties, when interacting with light elements strange properties can occur.

## Light Elements

Regular elements, pure logic.

## Threshold

The threshold was created by having the energy of the light sun be forced into one point inside itself, then the same happens with the dark sun, and on the inside the energy is colliding and forming Midpoint.

The threshold is merely the stable energy forming a small area that can be travelled through.

## Midpoint

Unstable energy from both suns, a result of the Threshold's creation.

## Light Realm

Ironically the realm that had the dark sun and is the opposite of the dark realm.

## Dark Realm

Ironically the realm that had the light sun and is the main realm.

## Suns switching realms

It is a normal occurrence.

It's just this time humans were around for it and actually saw it.
