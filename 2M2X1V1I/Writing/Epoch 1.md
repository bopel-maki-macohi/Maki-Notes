# Chapter 1 : Invoy

Lozen, March 2029\.

Lasha is on a stroll.  
Then a guy flies out of an alleyway, a rock embedded into his body.  
Lasha quickly runs past said alleyway, a beeping object inching closer behind her.

Blowing up behind her, Lasha has to hold onto a pole to not fly away. Once the explosion settles Lasha complains in her head about nothing being done about the thugs.

Once Lasha gets home, Niute shows off their new work in progress project: Restraint Blasters.  
Niute complains about them taking a while to work on due to quality of life features.  
Lasha offers to help with ideas and Niute agrees.

Days later.  
Niute shouts his completion of the Restraint Blasters.  
Eventually he and Lasha agree on her being the hero of Lozen using the Restraint Blasters.  
She needs a suit first.

Later that same night.  
A fight in a bar on the north quadrant of Lozen has begun between 2 gangs.  
Several deaths occur.  
But right as the leaders of the gangs are about to end it.  
The doors to the bar are slammed open and a figure flips around.  
Firing out restraints onto the criminals.

This is Lasha, Invoy.  
The protecting messenger of Lozen.

# Chapter 2 : Kaven Varlet

Lozen, April 2029

A well known strong man is in the streets of Lozen in a suit of armor causing havoc.  
Lasha, against Niute’s wishes, goes out and Invoy arrives on the scene.  
Invoy blasts Restraints at the man but he breaks through them with ease.  
Invoy doesn’t have enough time to retreat before she is ragdolled.

Invoy blasts out her last loaded Restraint at the man.  
Taking advantage of the man's blindness, Invoy retreats home.

Lasha arrives home, and goes to Niute, begging for upgrades to the Restraints strength.  
Niute begrudgingly agrees and goes to work on it.  
Lasha passes out and falls asleep.

A day later, night is about to arrive.  
Niute has finished the stronger Restraints and has already made multiple cartridges for Lasha.  
Lasha wakes up, well rested and confident.  
Explosions begin once more, and Invoy is on the way.

Right as the man is about to start causing more havoc.  
Invoy arrives, blasting Restraints onto his arms and his torso.  
Confident as ever the man tries to break free but fails.  
Invoy sighs in relief, and calls the cops.

# Chapter 3 : Power Jig

Lozen, May 2029\.

Two weeks ago, a giant water monster.  
Last week, a giant fire monster.  
But a constant is this person, Power Jig.

Lasha has been investigating this “superhero” ever since last week.  
Every bit of footage on the events has been scrolled through and analyzed.  
The areas of attack have been analyzed.  
There’s no evidence of them ever being there at all.  
Niute watches Lasha work on it, not a single bit of joy in his eyes.

An explosion.  
A giant rock monster.  
Lasha gets suited up.  
Niute frowns.

Invoy leaps out one of their windows, roof to lamp to lamp to lamp to roof.  
Invoy  attempts to jump onto the monster but fails, falling right through.  
The rock monster’s right shoulder then began to fall apart after that.  
Invoy  is then caught by Power Jig and brought to safety.  
Power Jig tells her to be safe and proceeds to stop the rock monster.

Later on.  
Lasha is explaining to Niute that the giant monsters must be illusions, holograms.  
Lasha asks if there is anything Niute can whip up to hide it.  
Niute grunts that it’s possible.  
Lasha begs for Niute to try and make it.  
Niute begins to work on it.

Days later.  
A new monster.  
An electricity giant.

Niute believes he is finished with the hologram counteraction machine.  
But the machine needs to lock onto the dispersed hologram waves to even begin.  
Lasha accepts the challenge. Niute doesn’t really like that.

Invoy arrives. Holding a machine.  
Invoy is “attacked” by the monster.  
Invoy weaves and dodges, getting closer and closer to the monster.  
Invoy leaps a large gap.  
Holding out the machine.  
The monster sends out a lightning bolt towards her.

The machine locks onto the signal.  
The hologram disappears.  
Several drones are circling the area where the monster was.  
Invoy smirks.  
Power Jig, standing on a drone, frowns.

A Restraint is blasted at Power Jig.  
Who is knocked off the drone.  
Grabbed by Invoy on a building below.

For “The Invoy”.  
2 months ago.  
On walls.  
Buildings.  
Streets.  
Corners.

People start dying.  
Bombs are planted and blown up.  
People just start disappearing.  
Lasha’s mind is collapsing.  
Niute just eats, letting it happen.  
Invoy starts appearing more and more and Lasha starts disappearing.

This person’s plan is working.  
They know it.  
Invoy knows it.  
She feels responsible for these people.

But she’s not.

She’s being fucked with.

Or they weren’t real at all.

Fake people.  
Fake bombs.  
What’s real anymore?

This mystery person fucked up.

Invoy sees them.

Invoy can go after them.

Building after building.  
No matter what is in her way.
