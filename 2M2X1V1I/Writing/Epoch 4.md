# Chapter 13 : The Bird

Jur Vali, Morln, November 2029

It’s been 2 months since Invoy was last seen, rumors spread she died after her mask was found in a destroyed base of operations found on the south side of Lozen. But Invoy wasn’t dead, she just moved on. There are no more than three people who know of that.

But they don’t care enough to share it.

Niute and Lasha. They’re enjoying themselves in the Jur Vali east half of Jur Valia Ontel, inside Morln. It’s a place Lasha’s not entirely foreign to but still is foreign enough to enjoy things that are normal to the residents of Morln. This was where they got married, Niute’s home. Good memories.

But not all good things can last.

There’s an attack on Jur Vali. Coming from Lozen, bulldozing through Jur Vali. It's Kaven Varlet, The Strong Man from April, he looks poisoned, veins and skin turning green, maybe that’s why he’s doing this. He screams saying he’s looking for a “Lex Breaker”, asking where he is.

Who the hell is that?

After getting back to their apartment, Lasha contemplates being Invoy again, the danger’s spreading from Lozen to elsewhere but she can’t be everywhere, she can’t protect everyone.

Niute says she shouldn't.

Lasha pleads.

“But what about the people?”

Niute shouted.

“FUCK THE PEOPLE.”

Lasha flinches.

Niute proceeds.

“Everytime you go out as Invoy I get left behind and end up just The Inventor making your weapons.”  
“It’s also dangerous as hell\! What if those giant hologram monsters weren’t holograms?”  
“You’d need hundreds of restraints to deal with them\!”  
“I get not a lick of credit, and my wife is in potential danger that could get her killed.”  
“You’re trying to be this tough hero, but then you're also trying to be my partner, try to live a normal life.”

Niute takes a couple seconds.

Breathes in.

And out.

His voice is now more concerned but still that bit of anger.  
He’s never been this mad before, for this long…

“Boy, girl, non-binary, whatever you wanna be.”  
“It doesn’t work.”  
“And I’m tired of trying to pretend it can, or that it does.”

Lasha and Niute pauses.

Lasha begins.

“Sorry.”  
“I just…”

But then she’s interrupted by an explosion caused by Kaven, buildings are collapsing, stores are being crushed, someone has to do something but what could the cops do?

Kaven is free, not a restraint on him.

But then suddenly, a blue blur zoomed by the window towards him and he started screaming, it sounded like he was zooming into the sky.

Lasha looks out the window, he’s gone…

“What?”

Suddenly the blue blur returns, zipping back to where they came.

“What??”

# Chapter 14 : The Plane

Jur Vali, Morln, November 2029

It’s been a week now since the blue blur appeared in Morln. Lasha’s been obsessed. She wants to know just who or what they are. Niute once again felt left behind and alone, returning back to Lozen, they can fix themselves when Lasha comes back.

Lasha is spending her time researching the blue blur. It’s so fascinating to her and she doesn’t even know why. It just seems to interest her an unnatural amount. Her life has been changing so much, changing how she acts, it’s so different.

It’ll probably be worth it.  
Eventually.

The blue blurs appeared several times in the past week. Lasha’s been there for most of them and she believes she’s got enough data to try and figure out where this blue blur originates. It always comes from the west and goes back west when it’s done, maybe originating in Jur Lavl?

Now the question, how to get in contact with it?

# Chapter 15 : The Super

Jur Vali, Morln, November 20th, 2029

“The Super” is the name the Morln news has given the blue blur. But what is its actual name? Super doesn’t feel right for it. Isn’t that a taken name anyway? Lasha internally swears she’s heard it before, referring to some dude in Baron.

Nevermind that.

It’s been another week and Lasha has prepared herself to venture off into Jur Lavi. The weather’s starting to get bad. The weather forecast said the wind’ll calm down but lightning storms are to be expected soon. Lasha needs to hurry.

Lasha made herself a new suit just in case anyone needs or sees her.

So it seems Invoy is back.

…

Seems.

As soon as Lasha leaves her apartment the wind and rain are a terrifying force, but she pushes through anyway, she uses her flexible nature to sneak through tiny areas to combat the wind.

Hours have probably passed. Lasha’s almost fallen asleep several times. It’s way too early for this. What was the last time she saw? Like 1am?

This was a bad idea.

“I’m so tired…”

That’s all Lasha can think of. Before she starts to fall.

Or fly. The wind might just end up dumping her in the sea. Who knows?

Who even cares?

Right before she falls asleep however there’s something in the distance.

Some hut.

Then something comes out.

A blur.

A blue blur.

# Chapter 16 : Loroc

Jur Vali, Morln, November 20th, 2029

Lasha wakes up in a sweat.  
Inside a poorly constructed home out of wood.  
The weather reports were right, the wind cleared but there’s thunder and lightning now.

She checks her watch, it’s 12pm.  
She checks her suit, not a single tear anywhere, the suit’s just about the same as she remembered it before she went to bed, albeit a bit wet, thank god  
Ok, that’s checked, but now where the hell is she?

Suddenly there is a blue blur.  
And there is a person inside now, male, sitting in front of her, who looks pretty young but his height makes her feel like he’s not that young.  
He honestly seems a bit older than her.

He waves.

Lasha hesitates but waves back.

Lasha realizes how close he is.

“Could you… move a little less close to me?”

He does so and scoots away enough for Lasha to stand, which Lasha does.

“Who are you?”

The man speeds away and brings back a whiteboard, it has “Loroc” written on it.

Why isn’t he just saying that?

“Why are you so silent?”

The “Loroc” on the whiteboard disappears and Loroc has changed the writing to “Mute”.

“Why?”

Loroc points out the window, zooming outside where Lasha can see her outside the window, Lasha looks, Loroc opens his mouth, facing away, then all of a sudden lava pours out of his mouth.

Oh, no wonder he did it outside.

“Uhm… Okay, what… where…”

Lasha doesn’t know what to say, it’s not everyday you get to meet a person and it turns out they have god damn super powers, Lasha recollects her thoughts and was about to continue the conversation, Loroc seems to be looking somewhere.

But then there is thunder, but it doesn’t sound exactly like thunder.

It’s shaking the ground.

Lasha sticks her head out the window, looking towards where Loroc is looking.

There’s a beam in the sky. A gold beam with green electricity circling it.

“Wanna go check that out?”

Loroc looks over at Lasha, who is smiling.

Loroc nods his head, lifts his arms out for Lasha to jump into, which she did.

Loroc then starts to speed over to the beam, going slow so Lasha doesn’t… well…

Die.
