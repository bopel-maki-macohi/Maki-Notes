# Chapter 1 : For The Sacred Ground

Ni Vohac, September 2029

A deal was attempted, only days ago.  
A man cloaked in yellow, approached The Knight Serviceman.  
Requesting access to Ni Vohac’s sacred metal, Colatin.  
Request denied, the man walked away, threatening a dark time coming for Ni Vohac.

This was overlooked  
But in the time since it does seem like that time is trying to approach.

Invaders.  
Machines.  
Machines with advanced fighting capabilities and weapons.

Ni Vohac’s Grade 1 Military Services cannot handle the machines.  
The Grade 2 Services are released.  
And the machines adapt.

The Knight Serviceman watches, and decides enough is enough.  
The 3rd Grade Military Service wouldn’t manage.  
He had to take care of this personally.

He leaps out from his throne, calling upon his Colatin Axe which flies towards his hand.  
He grabs his axe.  
Winding up.  
And he slams the axe into the ground.

A shockwave expands.  
The machines are flown away.  
Imploding on themselves.

TKS approaches the balled up remains of one of the machines.  
Proceeds to grab the machine and return to his throne.  
They are going to find the origins of these machines.

# Chapter 2 : Colatin Theft

It’s been about a week or so.

Peace has been partially restored.  
Fear still lives in the city.  
The Machine's remains have been cleaned up.  
So have the remains of the Vohan Graded Military Soldiers.  
They will be honored for as long as Ni Vohac lives.

Ni Vohac’s finest Vohan Researchers are looking into the Fighting Invasion Machines.  
They haven’t been able to find anything.  
The Knight Serviceman decides to forget about it and to discard the machines.  
But just as the words finished being spoken the machines beamed to life.

A blinding yellow light emerged in the room.  
No one could see and the machines snuck off.  
TKS screams for them to be found immediately.  
Who knows what they could be after.

Several Vohan citizens of various roles were searching for these machines.  
Some of them went into the Private Colatin Reserve Room and found the machines.  
But most of them were murdered before TKS could even be told of the discovery.  
When TKS arrived the machines had already escaped.

TKS went outside and saw the machines flying away.  
TKS immediately commandeered a vehicle and chased after them.  
A quarter of the Colatin reserves were taken.  
Just that alone is a dangerous amount.

TKS continues following the machines, for several minutes, keeping pace.  
The terrain starts getting wild, bumpy.  
Mountains, hills, they are everywhere.  
TKS accidentally runs into a hill and his commandeered vehicle explodes.

TKS walked out of the explosion.  
Patting the fire off his outfit.  
He walks around the hill.  
Seeing the machine fly into a city.

Tempo City.

# Chapter 3 : The Speed City

Tempo City, October 2029

It’s been some time since The Knight Serviceman arrived in Tempo City.  
He is searching for the machine that stole a quarter of the Colatin reserves in Ni Vohac.

But it’s been a few days.  
The machine could be long gone.  
TKS’s resolve is dying.

He’s taken the alias of “Warnit” in this place.  
It’s his old name from before he was The Knight Serviceman.  
Hearing it feels wrong nowadays.  
It’s like calling a parent by their actual name. 

Nevermind that.  
Warnit is on a mission.  
If he can’t find the machine.  
He will retreat back to Ni Vohac.

He spent his time exploring the entire city, every building, corner, alley.  
Every bit of the city.  
He found nothing.  
But then he found something.  
An impassable river.  
But a bridge.  
A bridge to Baron.

There's several foundations and companies there.  
They all have access to technology.  
Technology that could fit the technology of the machine.  
Freefall is there.

Freefall created that damn machine.  
They’re using the Colatin.  
They have the man cloaked in yellow.

# Chapter 4 : Speed vs Tactic

Baron, October 2029

At the Freefall Foundation, Warnit has arrived.  
Equipping his armor once more he prepares for battle.

From above lands The Man Cloaked In Yellow.  
Taunting him.  
Warnit charges at The Man, who dodges with efficiency.

Warnit cannot land blows.  
Warnit cannot land strikes.  
The Man dodges.  
Each and every time.

Warnit begins to use blasts.  
The foundation starts collapsing.  
The Man shoves Warnit into the collapsing building.

Warnit emerges but The Man is nowhere to be found.  
There is a new variable though.  
Multiple.  
The Freefall Foundation Members.

One of them is a speedster and charges at Warnit.  
Warnit blocks and dodges.  
Warnit strikes with his axe.  
The speedster phases through it and punches Warnit right in the face.

Warnit spins with his axe.  
Winding up.  
He tosses it directly at the speedster.  
The speedster phases again.  
But Warnit calls the axe back.  
One of the Freefall members screams out warning the speedster.

“SINCO!”
