## Chapter 1 : Radiation Reports

Lozen, July, 2097

Inside a Lozen apartment about 10 stories high, Dot sits in a chair, reading The Lozen Newsletter, she can't help but to fidget in her chair several times out of boredom, Dot turns to the next pace and notices a headline.

"The Trilands releasing ancient poison! Radiation levels off the charts!"

It speaks of how a Lex Breaker research team flew over the plots of land during a routine trip and detected high amounts of radiation coming from the lands, one of their team members were affected by it and are currently in a Jur Lavi hospital being treated.

Dot stares at the text, her fidgeting scratched to a halt.

Dot : "Kijo, are you there?"

Beside Dot in the chair appears a black goo that spikes up from the left chair arm, morphing into the head of Kijo.

Kijo : "I'm about 76% of the earth Dot, I'm almost everywhere."

Dot points at the newsletter.

Dot : "Are you in any of the Trilands?"

Kijo : "No..."

Dot : "Well we should probably go check it out."

Dot : "Apparently there's some radiation shi-"

Kijo : "*ahem*"

Dot rolls her eyes.

Dot : "Apparently there's some radiation *crap* going on with it."

Kijo nods her head, Dot rolls her eyes again.

Kijo : "Anyway, uhm..."

Kijo : "Yeah probably."

Kijo : "It mentions Lex Breaker, so, he probably has something to do with it."

Dot : "Good point."

Dot : "Where's Ainmore?"

Kijo : "Flying around The Nation."

Dot : "W-"

Kijo interrupts her.

Kijo : "I don't know why."
